The Ascendant System

TTRPG Production Studio

This is a small, privately operated tabletop roleplaying game document-production project. Its Google Drive integration allows its authorized owner to store versioned PDF review proofs and accompanying quality-assurance reports in a designated Drive folder.

The application uses Google OAuth only when its owner grants permission. It does not offer public sign-in or third-party accounts. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

For questions about Google Drive access or removal, contact jthiboust2@gmail.com.

This public informational website does not host the project's private source files, documents or credentials.
